# ADR 0003: Human approval for external actions

Status: Accepted

## Context
Incorrect applications and unsolicited outreach can harm the candidate. Websites may restrict automation. Legal declarations require user accuracy.

## Decision
No application submission or outreach sending without explicit approval of the exact payload. MVP provides manual submission packages and draft-only outreach.

## Consequences
Less automation, substantially lower legal, reputational, and data-integrity risk.
