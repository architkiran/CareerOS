# Build CareerOS around evidence and control

CareerOS should become a coherent **application workbench backed by complete candidate evidence**, with specialized agents producing inspectable results. The strongest lessons from Tsenta, Teal, Huntr and additional public repositories concern preserving source content, making changes reversible and connecting each task to the current application. More agent names cannot repair missing master-resume bullets or uncertain posting dates. Prioritize the full master inventory, editable tailoring and coverage checks, then honest monitoring and contextual networking. Commercial products are workflow references, not proposed runtime dependencies. The recommendations below preserve local storage and a zero-dollar runtime; they do not claim every feature is already implemented or independently benchmarked.

## Preserve the master before optimizing the match

Tsenta documents highlighted changes, direct editing, chat, an original-resume option and persistent preferences. Teal documents a reusable bullet bank, per-resume selection and an explicit distinction between editing one copy and saving changes across all resumes. Huntr separates comprehensive base resumes from tailored copies and evaluates base completeness separately from job alignment. The resulting product rule is clear: **tailoring changes a controlled copy; it does not silently erase the source inventory**. These are documented vendor workflows, not proven accuracy or hiring outcomes. ([Tsenta](https://help.tsenta.com/resume-tailoring-and-cover-letters), [Teal](https://help.tealhq.com/en/articles/14435954-how-to-edit-resume-bullets-in-teal), [Huntr](https://help.huntr.co/en/articles/12824077-understanding-huntr-s-resume-builder))

CareerOS should retain the full LaTeX source, its hash and baseline PDF, then map editable summary, experience, project and skill slots. Preserve every master experience and substantive bullet by default. Show the complete project bank separately from the selected projects, with pin, exclude, restore and relevance explanations. Import should distinguish source material, verified facts and unresolved conflicts: extracting a PDF does not itself verify its claims. A content inventory should expose missing mappings before selection begins.

YAMLResume separates schema-validated content from layout; RenderCV supports versionable source and multiple rendered outputs. RenderCV currently documents Typst output, so it is not an arbitrary-LaTeX patcher. OpenResume offers local editing, PDF updates and parser inspection, although its parser targets English single-column resumes. These support content/presentation separation, without guaranteeing preservation of every custom template. ([YAMLResume](https://github.com/yamlresume/yamlresume), [RenderCV](https://github.com/rendercv/rendercv), [OpenResume](https://github.com/xitanggg/open-resume))

## Make tailoring an editable evidence decision

A focused workspace should connect **Requirements, Content and Preview**, retaining the role, employer and version as stable context. Requirements need JD excerpts and explicit-versus-inferred labels. Content needs complete experience bodies, the full project inventory, verified skill choices and editable summary proposals. Preview must show the actual PDF, a text diff, restore-original action and export state. Requests such as “keep Spark” or “use reconciliation” should persist with that version; lasting preferences need an explicit separate scope.

A JD evidence agent should map requirements to verified outcomes with supported, partial and missing states. An optional local Ollama model can propose typed patches containing the target claim, replacement, evidence references and rationale. **Schema validation controls structure, not truth**: metrics, dates, technologies, ownership and work-authorization assertions still require independent checks. Unsupported additions remain pending facts or rejected edits. Without a local model, label selection and reordering honestly. ([Ollama structured outputs](https://docs.ollama.com/capabilities/structured-outputs))

A layout and coverage agent should compare master and selected experience counts, bullets, extracted PDF text, required sections, overflow and unexplained underfill. An empty experience body or drastic reduction requires attention even when a PDF fits one page. Immutable artifact hashes and exact-version review protect approved materials. JobCtrl documents fact-linked validation and availability rechecks, but its paid-provider and scraping defaults should not be adopted. ([JobCtrl](https://github.com/ebarti/JobCtrl))

## Measure freshness instead of promising instant discovery

Separate **publication, source update, first observation and last successful check**. “Found today” must not become “posted today.” Greenhouse examples distinguish `updated_at` from `first_published`; missing publication time should remain unknown. Ashby's `publishedAt` means last publication and can reflect republishing. A “published within 24 hours” filter should require a supported timestamp and show its basis. ([Greenhouse](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board), [Ashby](https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api))

Monitoring should show cadence, next due, last success, stale-worker state, errors and provider delay. Stagger checks, respect blocking and retry instructions, and deduplicate local notifications by stable posting identity. Initial imports should establish a baseline or digest, not generate thousands of false fresh-posting alerts. Monitoring runs while the local application and worker run; a sleeping laptop cannot guarantee continuous observation. No reviewed source established universal public push delivery or minute-level discovery guarantees.

Provider limits matter. Remotive deliberately delays listings by 24 hours and advises at most four fetches daily. Jobicy allows synchronization no more than hourly and offers status checks for up to 100 stored IDs; absence from its rolling feed does not prove closure. WWR expressly permits public RSS reuse with backlink attribution. Himalayas provides unauthenticated remote-job coverage but refreshes daily. USAJOBS Search requires a key and identifying email, so it should remain optional without existing credentials. ([Remotive](https://github.com/remotive-com/remote-jobs-api), [Jobicy](https://github.com/Jobicy/remote-jobs-api), [WWR](https://weworkremotely.com/remote-job-rss-feed), [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app/api), [USAJOBS](https://developer.usajobs.gov/guides/authentication))

## Put networking inside each application

Simplify embeds referral research in the job/application workflow and distinguishes possible connections from confirmed referrals. CareerOS should adopt that placement with explicit states: research target, unverified lead, verified person and verified suitable recipient address. Preserve public source URLs, excerpts, checked time and uncertainty. No reviewed public ATS API supplies a dependable universal hiring-manager directory. ([Simplify](https://help.simplify.jobs/help/articles/4158996-find-referral-connections-with-simplify))

A networking agent should identify useful functions—role recruiter, relevant team lead, BU alumnus—and prepare a company-specific email, connection note and follow-up grounded in the actual role and verified projects. Names and addresses remain absent without evidence. Support, scam-report or agency-restricted inboxes are not automatically outreach targets. Search links and a research brief remain useful when no person is verified. LinkedIn and Handshake can contribute user-assisted searches and imports; their terms restrict unauthorized scraping and automated collection. A draft never implies permission to send. ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement), [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com/legal/tos/))

## Ship reliable handoffs before expanding autonomy

Begin with master ingestion and loss-of-content regression tests, continue through section editing, selection controls, saved feedback and coverage QA, then add timestamp filters, monitoring and local alerts. Evidence mapping, networking and availability checks should run through existing durable orchestration. Each agent needs an input hash, saved artifact, actual status, error state and useful next action. More frameworks or navigation destinations need a concrete justification.

Retain three primary destinations and use compact contextual controls, status text and accessible focus states. Jobtra demonstrates a cohesive FastAPI/SQLite workflow. Job Search Terminal's design guide recommends calm scanning, although its noncommercial license makes “source-available” more accurate than OSI-open-source. These references support improving the existing stack rather than assuming a rewrite creates polish. ([Jobtra](https://github.com/CU1KNIGHT/Jobtra), [Job Search Terminal design](https://github.com/uxdesignlab/job-search-terminal/blob/main/docs/design-system.md))

CareerOS's advantage can be answering four questions clearly: what changed, which evidence supports it, when the opportunity was observed or published, and what remains for the user to decide. That makes automation assessable despite incomplete coverage, contacts or model quality. Perfect tailoring, universal contact discovery and instant all-board monitoring remain unsupported promises; visible evidence and recoverable workflows are achievable standards.
